# Grocery Store Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Grocery Store Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard transforms retail sales data into actionable business insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor sales performance, analyze customer behavior, evaluate product categories, and identify growth opportunities across different regions and store formats.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data across multiple products, stores, and regions. However, extracting meaningful insights from raw data can be challenging.

This dashboard was designed to:

* Monitor key sales and operational KPIs
* Analyze product and category performance
* Evaluate sales distribution across locations
* Compare store performance
* Track customer satisfaction metrics
* Support strategic business decisions through interactive reporting

The dataset was prepared using Power Query by:

* Cleaning and validating records
* Handling missing and inconsistent values
* Standardizing category and location data
* Creating calculated fields
* Optimizing data structure for reporting

## DAX Measures Implemented

The dashboard utilizes DAX to calculate key business metrics, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Sales
* Average Rating
* Sales Contribution %
* Country-wise Sales Analysis
* Target Achievement Metrics
* Store Performance Indicators

* Total Sales
* Total Orders

* Country-wise Revenue
* Store Type Performance
* Target vs Achieved Sales

---

## Key Business Insights

* Generated over **1.2M in total sales** across all store formats.
* Significant sales contribution observed from France and Australia.
* Supermarkets contributed the highest share of overall revenue.
* Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods emerged as the leading sales categories.
* Tier-3 cities show highest sales performance
